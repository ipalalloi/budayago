# Sulbar Culture Knowledge — Product Hypothesis and Cultural Governance (Draft v0.1)

**Status: concept only.** No claim of an existing digital product, cultural endorsement, 2026 competition eligibility, school partnership or deployed AI service is made.

## Product hypothesis
A community-governed learning platform that turns **approved** knowledge shared by recognized cultural knowledge holders into structured, contextual, age-appropriate learning experiences for youth, teachers, and communities in West Sulawesi.

**Not** a public archive of all recordings, an AI replacement for knowledge holders, or an automatic license for traditional knowledge.

## User need / value proposition (to validate)
- Knowledge holders: control what can be recorded, quoted, adapted, taught, made public or withdrawn from future circulation.
- Students: contextual lessons, interactive comprehension and teacher-guided learning rather than passive viewing.
- Teachers and schools: reviewable modules, references, clear permissions and observable learning outcomes.
- Cultural communities: correct attribution, feedback, visibility into usage and benefit-sharing where applicable.

## Proposed minimum functional product (NOT YET BUILT OR VERIFIED)
1. Curated knowledge entry with provenance, holder sign-off, revision history and access scope.
2. Lesson builder: approved narratives -> learning objectives -> examples -> formative questions -> teacher review.
3. Student lesson player with checks for comprehension and correction/feedback.
4. Access controls, consent and publication review; separate private evidence store from public codebase.
5. Optional AI Q&A using ONLY approved content, source-linked answers, human correction workflow; do not claim AI accuracy until evaluation.
6. Basic anonymized learning analytics with data minimization.

## Cultural safety / permission gates
- Respect UNESCO's *Ethical Principles for Safeguarding Intangible Cultural Heritage*, particularly community leadership, free/prior/sustained/informed consent, respect for customary access limits, protection of material and moral interests.
- A recognized cultural holder/representative must validate the name of the tradition, role and authority of informants, historical narrative, contextual correctness, attribution and what must stay restricted.
- Permission matrix **per asset and use**: collection; internal analysis; lesson use; online display; AI retrieval; adaptation; competition promotion; further commercial use. Do not treat one permission as blanket consent.
- Access classes: PUBLIC_APPROVED, SCHOOL_APPROVED, COMMUNITY_ONLY, RESTRICTED / SACRED (NOT INGESTED into public repo or AI), WITHDRAWN_PENDING_REVIEW.
- Source labels: ORAL_VERIFIED, ARCHIVAL_VERIFIED, SCHOLARLY_SECONDARY, COMMUNITY_DISPUTED, UNVERIFIED. No content goes live from UNVERIFIED.
- Keep identifiable participant data and images/recordings of minors out of GitHub, public demo and AI prompts unless specific legal requirements and permissions are met.
- Maintain rights and benefit-sharing conversations before commercial reuse; don't presume the team owns community heritage.

## Unverified pilot candidates
Potential cultural locations, positions, living practitioners, tradition descriptions, rituals, teaching levels and school contacts remain **candidates** pending community consultation and documentation. A published historical source on Batetangnga describes *Ulu Bate* in a territorial framework; it does **not** establish that the term is a person's name or that any prospective informant is authorized for this project. Source for review: https://jurnalpangadereng.kemdikbud.go.id/index.php/pangadereng/article/download/198/pdf (Abdul Hafid & Sahajuddin, 2021). Confirm locally; do not infer sacred practice, lyrics or historical narrative from general web pages.

## Evaluation (proposed, not measured)
- Product: end-to-end lesson completion success, defect rate, task time, usability and accessibility tests.
- Learning: pre/post matched comprehension measures for consented pilot cohort; report sample size, instrument, missing data and limitations. Never invent learning gains.
- Culture: holder approval rate, correction turnaround, inappropriate-disclosure incidents, revocation handling.
- Sustainability: service maintenance cost, verified adoption, authorized institutional partnerships, equitable compensation/benefit agreement if applicable.

## Sustainable business model (hypotheses, not actual sales)
Consider school/institution service agreements, paid onboarding/training to authorized institutions, optional ethical culture-led programs and grants; keep core access affordable and preserve community veto over sensitive content. Track actual costs for preservation, review and technical operations. BMC's 9 components to be completed after interviews and budget checks.
